3. Theoretical Reasoning & Empirical Analysis

Regularisation (L1 vs L2)

• Ridge (L2) shrinks correlated coefficients smoothly toward zero but retains all features, tackling multicollinearity without dropping information.
• Lasso (L1) produces sparse models by zeroing out 15 redundant features at diamond corners of the L1 penalty ball, acting as embedded feature selection.
• Elastic Net combines both to group correlated predictors while maintaining sparsity.

Bias-Variance Trade-Off

As regularisation hyperparameter \(\alpha\) increases from 0.01 to 1000:
• Training error increases monotonically (higher bias).
• Test error initially decreases due to reduced variance before rising as underfitting dominates.
• The optimal \(\alpha\) minimizes total generalization error on unobserved test splits.

Feature Scaling Impact

Without StandardScaler (\(\mu=0, \sigma=1\)), features with large scales (like CodingTestScore up to 100) receive disproportionate shrinkage penalty compared to CGPA (0–10).
Standardizing features ensures balanced gradient updates and equitable regularization penalties across all predictors.
